Quis Primus

Latin for “Who's first?” 🏆

Quis Primus is a YouTube channel that turns real-world statistics into short animated rankings: 3D countdowns to #1 and racing bar charts about population, economies, energy, health and more.

Every number comes from public, trusted sources such as the United Nations, the World Bank and Our World in Data, and every video shows its source on screen.

Quis Primus Uploader

The channel is produced by an automated pipeline. Its only connection to YouTube is the Quis Primus Uploader, which uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos to the channel owner's account. It does not access, collect or store data about any other user.
